Even if you don’t use one of the crosshairs listed above, there are a few tips that you should keep in mind when creating ther perfect crosshair for you in CS2.

Static vs Dynamic: We strongly recommend using a static crosshair, which will prevent it moving when you are walking or sprinting. If you are brand new to CS, it may be helpful to use dynamic – to remind you about movement innaccuracy. But, once you have the hang of it, a static crosshair will typically be much easier to control recoil, when spraying.

Follow recoil: Speaking of recoil, CS2 has a new “follow recoil” setting, which will track where the next bullet in a spray will land. Although some players have dabbled with it, it’s very rarely used among top players, which indicates it’s probably not optimal, unless you are really trying to master spray control.

Size and color: When it comes to the size and color of your crosshair, of course this is personal preference. However, cyan is the most popular color, as it stands out against almost all backgrounds. Green and Yellow are also popular choices. Red and dark blue is less common, as it can be less visible in dark areas of maps.

For the size, remember that the resolution and aspect ratio you play on will impact how large the crosshair appears on your screen, so if you copy a pro player’s code, it will appear differently if your display settings are different.
