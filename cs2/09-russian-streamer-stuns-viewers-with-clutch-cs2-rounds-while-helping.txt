Russian Twitch streamer k0lixx has impressed viewers by winning a Counter-Strike 2 round while stepping away from his computer to help a customer at work.

Most CS2 players would prefer their teammates to stay at their keyboards during a round. For k0lixx, however, going AFK is part of juggling the game with customers collecting their orders.

The streamer broadcasts from a parcel pickup point, with an overhead camera showing the work area alongside his gameplay. Viewers can watch him grab items and serve customers before returning to deal with whoever is waiting on the server.

His Twitch stream titles lean into the unusual setup, with one translating to “I’m better at handing out orders at the pickup point than playing CS.” Judging by the clips, he’s managing both pretty well.

In one standout clip, k0lixx secures a kill before leaving his character standing still while he retrieves items and helps a customer.

After finishing, he returns to his computer and takes out another two opponents to win the round. The successful juggling act sends him into celebration, with the streamer popping off and dancing.

The moment made its way to Reddit, where viewers were impressed by his multitasking.

“Someone call his manager……and tell him how good his staff are,” one commenter joked. Another wrote: “Lmao this is a crazy stream setup I love it.”

“Promote him,” someone else chimed in.

It isn’t his only example of squeezing kills between customer interactions, either.

In another clip, he is gathering items for a customer when he spots an opponent on his screen. He quickly secures the kill before returning to help them, then rushes back after hearing something through his headset to rack up two more frags.
