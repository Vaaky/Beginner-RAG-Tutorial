For some reason, chickens have been added to CS2, bringing adorable animals into the extremely popular FPS game. So, here’s all you need to know about them.

Whether you’re looking to grab a new pet after chickens were shadowdropped into CS2, or just want to see what this random update is all about, chickens are shrouded in mystery thanks to Valve not acknowledging their addition to the game at all.

So, if you want to know all there is to know about chickens in CS2, here are all the available options, their accessories, how to hatch them, and more.

Currently, we know of three different variants of chickens in CS2. Each comes with a variety of different colors according to CS2Locker:

Currently, many have been finding Chicken Eggs in their Weekly Drops, with the egg appearing in both of your two reward slots and showing up next to you in the main menu, where you can inspect it.

So, it seems to be a random pull in the Weekly Drop.

Once you have the egg, you’ll need to simply wait. Fans have reported chicks to hatch automatically around a week after they got it. When it does hatch, it’ll turn into a chick.

It’s also important to note that when it has hatched, you need to feed your chick regularly, as it won’t grow up if you forget.

Chickens need to eat if they’re going to grow big and strong, and that’s no different in CS2. To find feed, you’ll need to grab it from the Weekly Care Packages, and it’s once again random, like the eggs.

You can get three different rewards: a 4lb bag, an 8lb bag, or two 8lb bags. Naturally, the latter will last longer.

You’ll want to focus on doing this as the chicken will leave if it gets too hungry. But the game will let you know when the chicken is hungry, so make sure you keep an eye out. It’ll also let you know when the food will expire.

Alongside the varous chickens you can get, CS2locker has reported that accessories will be available too, though their use in matches is unconfirmed.
