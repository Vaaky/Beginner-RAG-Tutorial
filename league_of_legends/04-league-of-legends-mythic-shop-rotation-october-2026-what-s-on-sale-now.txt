Here's the current LoL Mythic shop rotation, as well as Riot's list of changes that are slated for patch 26.22, completely reworking the store.

Focused on slightly higher-grade cosmetics than the general store, the League of Legends Mythic shop is where you'll find Prestige skins from previous updates, special chromas, and various icons and emotes, all available for Mythic Essence (or Purple Essence, if you prefer to go by color). It has various sections, all of which update at different times, so there's quite a lot to keep track of. We're here to help, however, so let's take a look at what's on offer during this rotation.

From patch 26.22 onwards, Riot has confirmed that the Mythic Store will now be unique to players, giving you skins that are better tailored to the champions you play. If you're looking to pick up any of the current roster, then, make sure you do so before patch 26.22 drops on Wednesday November 4.

As you can see above, everything in the Mythic store requires you to spend Mythic Essence, but it isn't exactly the easiest thing to obtain. While disenchanting a champion shard will net you some Blue Essence, and getting rid of a skin shard will give Orange Essence, Mythic Essence is much less predictable and requires either a bit of grinding or a particularly healthy bank account.
