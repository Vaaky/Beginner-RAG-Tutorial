Our Valorant tier list will get you up to speed with the best agents available in Season 2026 Act 3, helping you rack up the Ws with ease.

Who are the best Valorant agents? Whether you're looking to top frag through ruthless aggression, keep a sharp eye on what your opponents are up to, or control the map with your util, our Valorant tier list will tell you who's a hotshot, and who's really not.

There are 29 Valorant characters as of Season 2026 Act 4, but not all of them are well-suited to tackle the current cut-throat pace of the game. During each Valorant act, different agents will have their time in the sun as fresh balance patches come into effect in the FPS game. To find out who tops our Valorant tier list right now, read on.

Based on our tier list, it's clear that Clove and Sage are the most consistent go-to picks for most, and it's unsurprising. Clove can frag out without fear of losing initial contact while her ultimate's available, similarly to Phoenix. However, putting Duelist-tier power in the hands of a Controller makes for a powerful chimera of an agent, and the fact that Clove can control her smokes even when she does go down provides amazing utility.

Sage, meanwhile, can be considered Valorant's ol' reliable for off-rolers. She's simple to pick up and play, but also provides all sorts of creative possibilities with her wall. In ranked play, where site executes tend to be disjointed, Sage can play into this with her slow orbs, buying time for your team to rotate on defense, or making it difficult for defenders to fast retake when you're on the attack. She's not as popular in high ELO, but she's a great pickup for the average player.

The Valorant ranks are the place for flexing, so it should be unsurprising that flashy Duelists like Jett, Raze, and Phoenix are both popular and performant. Everyone wants to be the next Tyson 'TenZ' Ngo, so Jett being the most-played agent in the game tracks - we're kind of sick of seeing her pop up in most games, admittedly.

When it comes to Inititators, Sova, Fade, and Gekko all provide great intel that is critical for making plays and winning games. Sova's part of Valorant's launch roster, and we can imagine the years of lineup learning is helping to maintain his popularity. It also helps that he's the best pick for most who are getting into the information-gathering game, with both his drone and recon dart providing plenty of juice. It's also incredibly fun to beam people with Hunter's Fury.

Finally, let's talk about the Brimmy with the Stimmy. Brimstone, similarly to Sage, can be considered the gateway into playing Controllers. These legacy agents have much simpler kits than their more modern counterparts, but that's not to disregard their potency. In Brimstone's case, his ultimate is fantastic for clearing sections of, if not entire sites - especially when used in tandem with his incendiary grenade. His smokes are nice and easy to use, and translate well to agents like Clove and Miks, which utilize similar mechanics for their own.

Now equipped with our Valorant tier list for ranked play, you should have no trouble blasting through enemies with a solid team of our top picks.
