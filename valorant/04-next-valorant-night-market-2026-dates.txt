All the latest news on the next Valorant Night Market, including how it works, eligible skins, and how to access your exclusive offers.

When is the next Valorant Night Market? Hopefully, some of your long-sought-after weapon skins have been added to your collection. If you're unsure whether to part with your valuable Valorant points, we can tell you how long you have to decide - and if you're disappointed by your market, how long we expect you to wait until the next one.

Of course, Valorant skins don't offer any competitive advantage, but looking cool and giving your opponents something pretty to look at as you dominate them in the FPS game offers its benefits. With anything from seasonal skins to colorful patterns and intimidating designs for most Valorant guns and blades, there's bound to be something you like in the next Valorant Night Market.

The Valorant Night Market is not open right now, with the latest period ending on October 6. We expect it to open again in early December.

Taking a closer look at dates from previous years across 2023, 2024, and 2025, there was one Night Market in fall, putting the current Night Market squarely in that frame. Last year, there was a Night Market that ran from mid-December through the new year.

Each Night Market offers you six random weapon skins to purchase at a discount, from the Select, Deluxe, and Premium tiers. You can also trade in a couple of old skins if you want. Your selection cannot be changed, won't include skins you already own, and will disappear after two weeks. Typically, there is only one Night Market per act, but every act since Episode 1, Act 3 has featured a Night Market, so they're consistent.

You can roll almost every skin line in the Valorant Night Market, though there are some notable exceptions. Exclusive and Ultra Edition skins, alongside skins released within two acts of the shop's opening, are excluded from the pool. Valid skins won't show until two acts after their initial release. For example, the Evori Dreamwings bundle was released in Episode 9, Act 1, so it wasn't available in the Night Market until Episode 9, Act 3.
