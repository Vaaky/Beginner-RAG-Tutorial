Riot Games has revealed Hush as the next agent coming to Valorant, a telekinetic Duelist originating from Guangdong, China.

Hush was unveiled with a cinematic trailer ahead of the VCT Champions 2026 grand final between LOUD and Team Vitality, and she’ll be playable when she joins the roster on October 27.

She’ll be the game’s ninth Duelist, joining an aggressive lineup that already includes the likes of Jett, Reyna and Raze.

Riot has now revealed Hush’s full kit, giving players a first look at how she’ll work in-game.

Her passive, Telekinesis, lets Hush pick up weapons and the Spike and interact with switches from an increased range.

Displacement (E), her signature ability, can be thrown or prepped, then reactivated to send it toward her crosshair. It explodes on impact or after a delay, pulling players to its center, and anyone pulled into a surface or another player is concussed. It also refreshes every two kills.

Flashbulb (Q) works in a similar way. It can be thrown, prepped and redirected toward her crosshair, then explodes on a surface or after a delay to flash nearby players.

For movement, Flow (C) instantly creates an anchor that Hush propels herself toward after a short delay. If she uses it in the air, she’ll briefly levitate in place before taking off.

Her ultimate, Suspension (X), is prepared instantly and sent forward, with Hush steering it using her crosshair. Reactivating it or firing again detonates it, levitating every player caught in the blast.
