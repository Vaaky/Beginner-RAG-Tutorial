With hundreds of unique Valorant skins to add to your favorite weapons at varying prices, here's every bundle and skin in one handy place.

Looking for a list of all the Valorant skins? It can be hard to keep track of Valorant's ever-growing skin catalog, but we've got you covered with our extensive library of every single one. Back when Valorant was Project: A and little was known about the game, Riot made one thing clear - it would be a game for skin collectors.

Since 2020, the game developer has actively been churning out cosmetics for one of the best PC games, and, Bandit aside, Valorant now has at least ten skins for every single weapon. But who's keeping count? Well, we are. If you're looking to plan your next Valorant purchase or prepare for the next Valorant Night Market, then keep reading.

All purchasable Valorant skins rotate in the in-game market. The four slots reset each day at 5pm PST / 8pm EST / 1am BST to include individual skins for random weapons. You can't buy a specific skin, so check the market daily. Notably, battle pass and time-limited skins cannot be purchased from the daily rotations. Agent contract skins, meanwhile, can be unlocked for free by progressing through each character's pass.

Time-limited bundles do not appear in the daily rotation or the Night Market. They are the ultimate Holy Grail for skin collectors who pay top dollar to stack their inventory with rare items.

The VCT 2026 collection featured a series of Classics themed around the VCT partner teams.

The VCT 2026 Season Capsule featured a pretty slick knife, with multiple color variants based on each VCT region.

The Run It Back Lunar 26 capsule brought with it some firm favorites, including the Gaia's Vengeance Ghost, Nocturnum Phantom, Aemondir Vandal, Forsaken Operator, and the Evori's Spellcaster melee skin.

V25's Run It Back collection featured another selection of popular skins that players will have eagerly been waiting for the return of in their shop rotations. This time, the bundle included the Kuronami Spectre, Nocturnum Bulldog, and EX.O Vandal.

The 2025 holiday collection brought sweet treats for players. The Cookie Kit Capsule featured the Mr. Sprinklebottom Buddy, Mr. Sprinklebottom flex, a Sprinkle Surprise player card, and the 'Gifted' title.

Designed for duos (*cough* e-daters *cough*), the Haz & Matt collection featured two gun buddies and player cards themed around the adorable duo. You also received two titles which are far more acceptable for pairing up than the litany of 'His X,' 'Her Y' IGNs plaguing the server. Cute.

2025's VCT Team Capsules marked a return for the themed Classics, which sport a unique inspect animation for each team alongside a VCT logo muzzle flash. As with other capsules, their contents can't be purchased separately.

Regional Capsules were made available for the Valorant Champions Tour in 2025, featuring a karambit, buddy, and player card themed around each of the four major VCT leagues: China, Pacifics, Americas, and EMEA. As of March 2025, over $25 million from the proceeds has been contributed to VCT partner teams.

Team Capsules were made available for the Valorant Champions Tour China, 2024. These Capsules featured a team-themed Classic skin, AR Inspect, a kill banner, SFX, a Gun Buddy and spray, and more, all featuring your favorite team's logos and colors. 50% of the purchase of these capsules also went straight to the team themselves.

The Ignite collection is only available on melee weapons and must be purchased as a whole bundle. The collection was released to celebrate the game's release in China. The bundle includes banners, cards, icons, and a gun buddy. Level up the weapon to trigger its animation and purple variant.

The first Arcane skin launched in Episode 3 alongside the release of the first series and included only the Sheriff weapon skin. In Episode 9, a skin was released for the Vandal, and a new melee weapon, the Arcane Gauntlets, was added.

Arcane can be upgraded to level two to unlock animation, VFX, and SFX, level three for an animation, and level four for a finisher. The Ep 3 bundle costs 2,380 VP, while the Episode 9 bundle is valued at 5,925 VP.

Valorant Champions 2021 bundle includes a Vandal skin and a karambit. In addition, the Vandal has four upgrade levels and once maximized, it glows in a golden aura for the match MVP. Valorant Champions 2021 bundle costs 6,264 VP.

For 2022, Riot took things to a new level by introducing kill-based animations. Valorant Champions bundle features a Phantom that glows with five kills, engulfing itself in a heavenly shimmer at 25 kills. The Phantom has four upgrade levels, whereas the melee has three. Valorant Champions 2022 costs 6,167 VP.
