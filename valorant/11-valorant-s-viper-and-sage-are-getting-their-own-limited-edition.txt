Valorant is turning Sage and Viper into limited-edition bubble teas, allowing fans to get their boba fix with drinks inspired by the two Agents.

The world of video game food collabs isn’t anything new. In fact, this year alone we’ve had everything from Umamusume branded vodka to Pokemon SPAM (yes, this is a real thing).

However, Valorant is teaming up with bubble tea branch The Alley to give Valorant fans in Germany the chance to drink boba beverages inspired by Sage and Viper.

The two drinks have been designed around the Agents’ abilities and designs, with The Alley stores across Germany offering Viper’s Sparkling Matcha Refresher and Sage’s Lavender Assam Milk Tea.

First up is Viper’s Sparkling Matcha Refresher, which leans into the Controller Agent’s toxic aesthetic with fresh cucumber slices designed to resemble green snake scales and her signature Toxic Screen.

The sparkling drink also features low-calorie crystal boba before finishing with the bitterness and aroma of matcha, which The Alley says reflects Viper’s mysterious character.

Meanwhile, Sage’s Lavender Assam Milk Tea combines rich Assam milk tea with lavender milk and long strips of brown sugar jelly. The latter is said to flow through the drink in a dragon-inspired pattern, adding a soft and chewy texture alongside a subtle caramel sweetness.

Both drinks will be available at three The Alley stores in Berlin, as well as locations in Dusseldorf, Munich, Kiel, Frankfurt, and Erfurt. Participating stores will also feature dedicated Valorant elements throughout the promotion.
