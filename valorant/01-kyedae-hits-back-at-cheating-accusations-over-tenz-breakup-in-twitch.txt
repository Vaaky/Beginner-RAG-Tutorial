Valorant star Kyedae has hit back at social media claims that her relationship with Tenson ‘TenZ’ Van Ngo ended due to cheating.

Back in February, long-time partners Kyedae and TenZ confirmed that their relationship had come to an end. Kyedae said that the pair had been “separated for some time” before the announcement, and that they had “stopped growing as individuals.”

As with all relationships that are put under a microscope online, fans searched for answers and didn’t believe the streamers. There have been insinuations on social media that cheating had taken place.

TenZ hit back at that, calling it “disgusting” and “blind hate” on the part of people accusing Kyedae.

Now that she has returned to Twitch after an 18-month break, Kyedae said that she has “done my part” in addressing the “narratives” that continue to circle her and TenZ.

“No, I did not get him to quit pro play. Guys, do we forget that I asked him to play an extra year and then he won Madrid?” she said regarding rumors that she asked TenZ to stop playing Valorant.

“It is, and was, sometimes frustrating when my name is literally being dragged through the mud over something that was completely mutual. That was insane. Tyson and I don’t have any ill intent towards each other, and I do wish him the absolute best,” Kyedae added.

The streamer also replied to X posts prior to her return to Twitch about things too.

“Me and John did nothing. I didn’t cheat. That was a narrative created by a redditor. They purposefully cropped out one of my best friends that was also on the trip with us who shared a room with him,” the Valorant streamer said herself on October 19.
